About Me

I am a Software Architect with 25+ years of experience in CAD platforms, computational geometry, distributed systems, and AI-driven engineering applications.

My expertise encompasses geometric modeling, CAD frameworks, variational solvers, graphics pipelines, scalable software architecture, and modern C++ development in Agile CI/CD environments.
