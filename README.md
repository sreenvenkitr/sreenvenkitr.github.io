## 👋 About Me
I am a Software Architect with **25+ years of experience** in CAD platforms, computational geometry, distributed systems, and AI-driven engineering applications. 

For over 18 years at **Autodesk**, I contributed to the architecture and evolution of *Fusion 360*—guiding it from its conceptual stage into a large-scale, cloud-connected CAD platform. My expertise lies in geometric modeling, CAD frameworks, variational solvers, graphics pipelines, scalable software architecture, and modern C++ development in Agile CI/CD environments.

## 🚀 Current Focus: GaussCol
I am currently exploring the intersection of 3D representations, geometric AI, and spatial intelligence through **GaussCol**, an independent research initiative. GaussCol is focused on transforming 3D Gaussian Splatting from a visualization representation into a foundation for interactive, collaborative spatial intelligence.

- 🔭 **[GaussCol Demo Repository](https://github.com/sreenvenkitr/GaussCol-demo)**: Check out the public demonstrations and experiments.
